What's in your report

  • When you trade, in market time. Every window is a New York session with your local hours beside it. If you trade from another time zone, this alone is usually worth it.
  • Your first trades of the day vs. the rest.
  • After a loss. Does the next trade get worse? What did re-entering within 15 minutes cost you?
  • Size. Entries far above your usual size, and adds to losing positions, each one listed.
  • How you exit. Median winner and loser in points and minutes. With minute bars: a replay of your entries with fixed brackets, scored on trades the bracket never saw, against your own exits.
  • Your written rules vs. your trades. Trend filters, VWAP bands, your own list, checked against the chart as it stood at the moment of each click. No look-ahead.
  • What the data can't tell you, said plainly. A model is trained on your older trades and tested on newer ones; if it's a coin flip, the report says so.
